Retatrutide GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon triple agonist

Retatrutide is an investigational triple agonist targeting the GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. Adding glucagon-receptor activity is studied for additional energy-expenditure effects on top of the incretin actions.

Dosage Chart (20 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 10,000 mcg/mL.

PhaseDoseVolumeU-100 units
Starting / low1 mg (1000 mcg)0.10 mL10 units
Standard / high2 mg (2000 mcg)0.20 mL20 units

Frequency: Once weekly, subcutaneous · Half-life: ~6 days

How it works

The three-receptor profile combines appetite and insulin effects (GLP-1/GIP) with glucagon-driven increases in energy expenditure, a combination under active research.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Triple-agonist mechanism studied for metabolic endpoints.
  • Once-weekly dosing.
  • Standard gradual escalation.

Notes

  • Investigational; escalate slowly.
  • 20 mg vial supports many weekly doses at research ranges.
