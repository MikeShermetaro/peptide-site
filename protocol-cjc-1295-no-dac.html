CJC-1295 (No DAC) Growth hormone releasing hormone analog

CJC-1295 without DAC (also called Mod GRF 1-29) is a growth-hormone releasing hormone analog. The 'No DAC' version has a short duration, producing a clean pulse of GH release, which is why it is frequently combined with a secretagogue like Ipamorelin.

Dosage Chart (10 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 5,000 mcg/mL.

PhaseDoseVolumeU-100 units
Starting / low0.1 mg (100 mcg)0.02 mL2 units

Frequency: 1-3x daily, subcutaneous · Half-life: ~30 minutes (No DAC form)

How it works

CJC-1295 binds the GHRH receptor on the pituitary, amplifying the natural signal to release growth hormone. Without the DAC modification, the effect is brief and pulse-like.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Produces a short, natural-pattern GH pulse in research settings.
  • Synergizes with ghrelin-mimetic secretagogues (Ipamorelin).
  • Short duration allows precise timing.

Notes

  • Best studied in combination protocols rather than alone.
  • Very small volumes per dose — measure carefully.
