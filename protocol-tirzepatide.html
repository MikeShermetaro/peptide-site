Tirzepatide GIP/GLP-1 dual receptor agonist

Tirzepatide is a dual agonist that activates both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors. The combined incretin action is studied for metabolic and weight-related endpoints, with once-weekly dosing and gradual escalation.

Dosage Chart (10 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 5,000 mcg/mL.

Frequency: Once weekly, subcutaneous · Half-life: ~5 days

How it works

Simultaneous GIP and GLP-1 receptor activation enhances insulin sensitivity and secretion, slows gastric emptying, and reduces appetite through complementary pathways.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Dual incretin action studied for strong metabolic effects.
  • Once-weekly administration.
  • Titration schedule standard to manage GI tolerability.

Notes

  • Like other incretins, start low and escalate.
  • GI symptoms most common during dose increases.
