CJC-1295 (No DAC) + Ipamorelin 10mg Blend Growth-hormone axis blend

This blend pairs a GHRH analog (CJC-1295 No DAC) with a ghrelin-mimetic secretagogue (Ipamorelin) in one vial. The two act on different receptors, which is why they are commonly combined in growth-hormone research protocols.

Dosage Chart (10 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 5,000 mcg/mL.

Frequency: 1-2x daily, subcutaneous · Half-life: Mixed (short)

How it works

CJC-1295 amplifies the GHRH signal while Ipamorelin acts on the ghrelin/GHS receptor; the combined effect is studied for a stronger, natural-pattern growth-hormone pulse than either alone.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Two complementary mechanisms in one vial.
  • Studied for amplified GH pulses.
  • Convenient single-injection protocol.

Notes

  • Timing before sleep is a common research variable.
  • Small per-dose volumes; measure carefully.
