GHK-Cu Copper-binding tripeptide

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide studied for skin, hair, and tissue-remodeling applications. It is used both in injectable research protocols and in topical formulations.

Dosage Chart (50 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 5 mL bacteriostatic water → 10,000 mcg/mL.

Frequency: Daily, subcutaneous or topical · Half-life: Short

How it works

GHK-Cu delivers copper to cells and is associated in research with modulation of genes involved in tissue remodeling, collagen synthesis, and antioxidant activity.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 5 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Studied for skin quality, wound support, and collagen synthesis.
  • Investigated for hair-follicle effects in topical use.
  • Antioxidant-associated activity in research.

Notes

  • Larger vial (50 mg) reconstituted in more water for practical concentration.
  • Also formulated topically outside injectable protocols.
