BPC-157 Synthetic pentadecapeptide

BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide derived from a sequence found in gastric juice. It is one of the most widely discussed research peptides for tissue support and is frequently paired with TB-500 in recovery-oriented protocols.

Dosage Chart (5 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 2,500 mcg/mL.

Frequency: Once or twice daily, subcutaneous · Half-life: Short (minutes to a few hours, route dependent)

How it works

Research suggests BPC-157 may influence angiogenesis and growth-factor signaling (for example the VEGF pathway), which are associated with tissue repair processes in preclinical models.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Studied for tendon, ligament, and soft-tissue support in animal models.
  • Investigated for gastrointestinal protective effects.
  • Commonly combined with TB-500 in recovery research stacks.

Notes

  • Dosing is typically expressed in micrograms; measure carefully on a U-100 syringe.
  • Human clinical evidence is limited; most data are preclinical.
