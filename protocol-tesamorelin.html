Tesamorelin Growth hormone releasing hormone analog

Tesamorelin is a stabilized GHRH analog studied for its effect on growth-hormone release, with particular research attention to visceral fat.

Dosage Chart (10 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 5,000 mcg/mL.

PhaseDoseVolumeU-100 units
Starting / low1 mg (1000 mcg)0.20 mL20 units
Standard / high2 mg (2000 mcg)0.40 mL40 units

Frequency: Once daily, subcutaneous · Half-life: ~30-40 minutes

How it works

As a GHRH analog, tesamorelin stimulates the pituitary to release growth hormone, which in turn raises IGF-1 and is associated with reductions in visceral adipose tissue in studies.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Studied specifically for visceral fat reduction.
  • Raises endogenous GH/IGF-1 in research.
  • Once-daily dosing.

Notes

  • IGF-1 is sometimes monitored in research contexts.
  • Reconstituted product is refrigerated.
