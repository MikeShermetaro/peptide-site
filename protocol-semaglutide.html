Semaglutide GLP-1 receptor agonist

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist widely studied for glucose regulation and appetite. Its long half-life supports once-weekly dosing, and research protocols typically escalate the dose gradually.

Dosage Chart (5 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 2,500 mcg/mL.

Frequency: Once weekly, subcutaneous · Half-life: ~7 days

How it works

By activating GLP-1 receptors, semaglutide enhances glucose-dependent insulin release, slows gastric emptying, and reduces appetite signaling in the brain.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Studied extensively for glycemic control and weight-related endpoints.
  • Once-weekly dosing due to long half-life.
  • Gradual dose escalation is standard to improve tolerability.

Notes

  • Gastrointestinal effects (nausea) are common early and usually ease with titration.
  • Dose is escalated over weeks rather than started at target.
