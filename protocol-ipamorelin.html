Ipamorelin Growth hormone secretagogue (ghrelin mimetic)

Ipamorelin is a selective growth-hormone secretagogue that stimulates the pituitary to release growth hormone. It is valued in research for being relatively selective, with limited effect on cortisol or prolactin compared with older secretagogues.

Dosage Chart (10 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 5,000 mcg/mL.

Frequency: 1-3x daily, subcutaneous · Half-life: ~2 hours

How it works

Ipamorelin binds the ghrelin/GHS receptor and prompts a pulse of endogenous growth hormone release, mimicking the body's natural pulsatile pattern.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Selective GH release with minimal effect on cortisol/prolactin in studies.
  • Commonly paired with CJC-1295 to combine a releasing peptide with a secretagogue.
  • Short half-life supports timed dosing (for example before sleep).

Notes

  • Timing around meals and sleep is a common research variable.
  • Often dosed in micrograms; a 10 mg vial lasts many doses.
