TB-500 Thymosin beta-4 fragment

TB-500 is a synthetic version of a region of the naturally occurring protein thymosin beta-4. It is studied for its role in cell migration and tissue repair, and is often used alongside BPC-157.

Dosage Chart (5 mg vial)

Reconstitute with 2 mL bacteriostatic water → 2,500 mcg/mL.

Frequency: 1-2x weekly (loading), subcutaneous · Half-life: Longer-acting than BPC-157

How it works

Thymosin beta-4 regulates actin, a key cytoskeletal protein involved in cell movement. This activity is associated in research with wound healing, new blood vessel formation, and reduced inflammation.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 2 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Studied for recovery and flexibility of connective tissue.
  • Investigated for promoting cell migration during repair.
  • Frequently stacked with BPC-157.

Notes

  • Often dosed on a weekly loading schedule rather than daily.
  • Reconstituted solution should be refrigerated and used within a few weeks.
