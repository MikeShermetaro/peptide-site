BPC-157 + TB-500 20mg Blend Recovery blend (two tissue-support peptides)

This blend combines BPC-157 and TB-500 in a single 20 mg vial, a common pairing in recovery-oriented research because the two peptides are studied for complementary tissue-support roles.

How it works

BPC-157 is associated with angiogenesis and growth-factor signaling, while TB-500 (a thymosin beta-4 fragment) is associated with actin regulation and cell migration. Together they are studied as a combined repair-support approach.

Reconstitution steps

  1. Let the vial reach room temperature (~15-20 min).
  2. Draw 3 mL bacteriostatic water and inject slowly down the vial wall.
  3. Swirl gently until clear; do not shake.
  4. Label, refrigerate (2-8°C), protect from light; use within a few weeks.

Potential benefits

  • Combines two of the most-studied recovery peptides.
  • Single vial simplifies a combined protocol.
  • Often used for connective-tissue research.

Notes

  • Because it is a blend, the per-dose amount covers both peptides together.
  • Refrigerate after reconstitution.
